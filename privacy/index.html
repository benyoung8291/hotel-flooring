Privacy policy

How Hotel Flooring, a Premrest business, handles the information you send us.

Request a quote 1300 207 915

This policy applies to https://hotelflooring.com.au, operated by Premier Restorations Group Pty Ltd (“Premrest”, “we”). Hotel Flooring is a Premrest business. It is written for Australian Privacy Principles. It is not legal advice.

What we collect

If you send the enquiry form or email us, we collect the details you provide: name, company, email, phone, city, project type, approximate area or rooms, timing, message, and the page you sent it from. If you call 1300 207 915, we collect what you tell us so we can respond.

Why we collect it

To respond to quotes and project enquiries, to contact you about hotel flooring supply or installation, and to keep a record of the brief you sent. We do not sell your details.

Where the form goes

The website form posts to Formspree (formspree.io) so the enquiry can be emailed to our projects inbox. Formspree’s processing is described in their own privacy documentation. Do not send payment card numbers or other sensitive information through the form.

Analytics

If a Google Analytics 4 tag is added to this site, it may collect standard usage data. A generate_lead event may fire when a form is sent successfully. No measurement ID is present until Premrest installs one.

Access and correction

To access or correct personal information we hold, email projects@premrest.com.au or write to Premier Restorations Group Pty Ltd, Australia. You may complain to us first; you may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

We may update this page when the site or our processes change. The canonical URL remains https://hotelflooring.com.au/privacy/.
