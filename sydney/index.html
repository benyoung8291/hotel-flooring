Sydney CBD, harbour precincts, airport and Greater Sydney

A Sydney hotel does not pause for a floor.

Harbour-front inventory is expensive to dark. CBD properties run conferences through the week. Airport houses turn on early flights. Serviced apartments in Parramatta still have residents on a 30-day stay. The flooring brief has to respect that.

We supply and install hotel carpet, corridor carpet, guest-room flooring and vinyl for Sydney hotels, motels and serviced apartments. Staging follows occupancy, not a wishful weekday programme. The same approach runs in Melbourne and Brisbane for groups that brief nationally.

What to send with a Sydney brief

City or suburb, number of rooms or corridor metres, any specified product, event blackout dates, and whether you want supply-only or supply-and-install. Night access and dock constraints help us price the programme, not just the metres.

Sydney hotel flooring questions

Which parts of Sydney do you cover for hotel flooring?

Sydney CBD, Circular Quay, Darling Harbour, the Rocks, Kings Cross and Potts Point, the airport corridor, Parramatta and Greater Sydney hotels and serviced apartments. Regional NSW is quoted by arrangement.

How do you work around harbour and CBD access?

Dock and lift windows are written into the staging plan. Public-area work is usually night or early morning. Rooms are released in blocks so the rest of the house stays on the books.

Can you match an existing hotel carpet colourway?

We work to the specified range — broadloom, Axminster or carpet tile — and can supply remaining rooms or a wing to the current colourway, or help you reset a floor when the old range has been retired.

Brief a Sydney hotel floor.

Tell us the property, the zones and the occupancy window. We will come back with a path to supply, install, or both.
