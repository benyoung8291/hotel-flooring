How an occupied hotel install runs.

Walk the property

Rooms, corridors, public areas, wet zones, lifts, docks and the nights you can actually give us.

Zone the spec

Product per area, transitions, spare material for later room repairs, and any brand-standard colourway.

Stage the programme

Floor by floor or room by room against occupancy, events and housekeeping. Night work where the public areas need it.

Prepare and lay

Uplift, subfloor, moisture and levelling first. Then carpet, tile, LVT or sheet vinyl, with guest-safe hoarding.

Hand back inventory

Clean rooms and corridors returned to sales. Care notes for housekeeping. A path for damaged-tile or room swaps later.

Hotel flooring questions

Can you install hotel flooring without closing floors to guests?

Yes. Most hotel work is staged room by room or floor by floor, often at night or in off-peak windows, so the rest of the property keeps selling. Guest-safe hoarding, low-odour systems and quiet methods are planned against your occupancy calendar.

Do you supply only, or supply and install?

Both. Hotel groups with in-house trades or a preferred installer can take supply-only. Most properties ask us to supply and install so product, preparation and programme sit in one place.

Which hotel areas do you floor?

Guest rooms, corridors, lobbies, ballrooms and function rooms, restaurants, back-of-house, wet areas and pool surrounds. Product follows the zone: broadloom or Axminster, carpet tiles, LVT, sheet vinyl and entrance matting.

Which cities do you cover?

Melbourne, Sydney and Brisbane are the primary hotel markets. Other Australian cities and regional properties are quoted by arrangement.

How do I get a quote?

Call 1300 207 915 or send the form with city, rooms or area, timing and any specified product. Drawings and a programme window help us respond with a clear next step.
