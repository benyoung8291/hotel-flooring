Brisbane hotel flooring questions

Do you cover the Gold Coast as well as Brisbane?

Brisbane CBD, South Bank, Fortitude Valley, the airport corridor and metro hotels are the core. Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast and regional Queensland hotel and motel work is quoted on a project basis.

Can a Brisbane motel replace rooms one at a time?

Yes. Owner-operators often release a handful of rooms in a soft week. Carpet tile is useful here because a damaged room can be repaired later without redoing the wing. See motel carpet replacement.

How do you handle Brisbane humidity and wet areas?

Subfloor moisture is checked before vinyl or LVT goes down. Bathrooms, laundry, kitchens and pool surrounds take sheet vinyl or slip-resistant systems specified for wet use — not leftover guest-room carpet.
